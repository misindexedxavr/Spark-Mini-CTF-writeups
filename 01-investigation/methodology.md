# Methodology

## Sources

The chain uses public reporting from SecurityWeek, KELA, InfoStealers by Hudson Rock, and KrebsOnSecurity. Each article is linked in [the source list](../03-evidence/sources.md).

No leak sites, stolen credential databases, private accounts, or direct contact with people are part of the intended solve path.

## Reproducing the chain

1. Read the SecurityWeek article in Challenge 01 and locate the public alias and country.
2. Read the KELA report in Challenge 02. Its title identifies the group and co-founder alias.
3. Read the InfoStealers article in Challenge 03. Check its headline and the section titled "The Infostealer Connection".
4. Read the KrebsOnSecurity article in Challenge 04 and identify the collective name.
5. Return to KELA's report and locate the phrase "previously known as".
6. Use the same KELA report to find the group's earlier name.

## Evidence handling

Each writeup records the source, the relevant public statement, the interpretation, and the normalized flag. Unnecessary details are not copied. In particular, these writeups do not reproduce personal information, credentials, or details that could identify a private individual.

## Confidence and attribution

A report that a group claimed an attack does not independently prove that the group carried it out. A report of an arrest or detention does not establish guilt. Answers are limited to the public labels explicitly requested by the challenges.
